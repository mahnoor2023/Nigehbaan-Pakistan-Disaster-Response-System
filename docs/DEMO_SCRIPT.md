# Judge Demo Script (5–7 minutes)

Keep the district selector on **Malir** for the mapped portion of the demo. Nigehbaan is a simulation prototype; do not describe its scenarios as live operations.

## 0:00–0:45 — Overview

Open **Overview** and select Malir. Point out the district, weather context and household/response indicators. Explain that Open-Meteo values are model output and forecasts, not station observations. If weather is unavailable, use the displayed unavailable state and continue.

## 0:45–1:30 — Coverage

Open **Coverage Map**. Show the available Malir geographic reference points and the map attribution. Explain that the other six districts intentionally use a non-map scenario, and that complete verified Karachi district polygons are not included. If tiles fail, the map reports the issue; use the district list to continue.

## 1:30–3:30 — Command Center

Open **Command Center**. Move the flood scenario control to a higher level. Select a highly ranked household and read its risk explanation. Review the suggested resource allocation and unmet demand. Explain that the ranking and dispatch plan are computed for this synthetic exercise and do not contact responders.

Create a mission for the selected household, choose a responder team, and submit. Advance the mission through the valid status actions. Point out that a completed demo mission does not imply an actual rescue or a shelter arrival.

## 3:30–4:30 — Households and Missions

Open **Households** to show the searchable priorities. Open **Rescue Missions** to show the locally recorded mission and its status. Explain that mission data is stored in this browser, not a shared backend.

## 4:30–5:30 — Shelters

Open **Shelters**. Identify Malir facilities as OSM facility candidates with unverified shelter use and estimated capacity. Increase occupancy manually to demonstrate capacity tracking. Explain that the mission did not automatically change occupancy.

## 5:30–6:15 — District isolation and close

Switch to Karachi West and show its separate synthetic, non-map scenario. Return to Malir and show its local mission and occupancy still present in the same browser. Close by describing the next data requirement: verified district geometry and validated location datasets, followed by an operationally governed backend before any real-world use.

## Presenter disclosures

- Household records, incidents, vehicles, capacities, allocations and ETAs are synthetic or simulated.
- Weather is model-based. Map points are sourced geographic references; candidate facilities are not verified shelters.
- Missions and occupancy are browser-local. No real dispatch, rescue confirmation, shelter arrival or live emergency feed occurs.
- If external weather or map tiles are unavailable, continue with the visible fallback and the non-map scenario controls.
